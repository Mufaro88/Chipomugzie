# Voice analysis skill

Study my real content and produce a voice profile the repurpose skill can use.

## Steps

1. **Read everything** in `voice/` (all subfolders, all files). If `voice/` is
   empty, tell me to paste my past posts/scripts in there first (see
   `voice/README.md`).

2. **Analyze** across all of it:
   - Hook / first-line patterns (contrarian claim, direct question,
     confession, bold stance, number, etc.)
   - Sentence rhythm and length.
   - Tone and register (casual, punchy, formal, playful).
   - Signature phrases and verbal tics.
   - Recurring themes and topics I lean on.

3. **Write** the findings into `context/brand-voice.md`, replacing the
   placeholder. Be specific and cite patterns with short examples pulled from
   my actual content.

4. **Report** back the 3 hooks I lean on most and any topic I talk about more
   than I probably realize.

## Note
If I don't have my own content yet, I can drop in content from creators I
admire instead, and you analyze theirs so I can learn from it — but label it
clearly as reference, not mine.
