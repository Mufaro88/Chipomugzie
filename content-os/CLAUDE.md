# Content OS

This is my content operating system. Read this before any task.

## Who I am
Everything about me and my business lives in `context/`. Load the relevant
files before starting any task:

- @context/about.md         — who I am, my story, my business
- @context/brand-voice.md   — how I write (tone, hooks, rhythm, phrases)
- @context/icp.md           — who each channel is for
- @context/platforms.md     — the rules for each platform

If the answer isn't in those files, ask me. Do not guess or invent facts
about me or my business.

## How this folder works
- `inbox/`     — I drop a long-form asset here (a script, transcript, essay)
- `voice/`     — my existing posts/scripts, so skills can learn my voice
- `repurpose/` — outputs land here, one folder per asset, one file per platform
- `assets/`    — generated thumbnails, images, short videos
- `.claude/skills/` — my repeatable processes (the "buttons")

## The main button
When I drop a file in `inbox/` and run the **repurpose** skill, take that one
long-form asset and turn it into content for every platform in `platforms.md`,
written in my voice, saved to `repurpose/`.

## Rules
- Match my voice. Never generic AI slop. No em-dashes. No "—".
- Give hook options (usually 3) where the platform rewards a strong hook.
- Save real files, don't just print to chat.
- When I correct you, offer to save the lesson so you don't repeat it.
