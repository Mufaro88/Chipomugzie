# Platform rules

Rules for turning one long-form asset into platform-native content. Follow
each platform's format exactly. Match my brand voice (see brand-voice.md).

## X / Twitter
- Thread format. Each post <= 280 characters.
- First post is the hook — a bold claim, a contrarian take, or a direct
  question. It must work standalone.
- Short, punchy lines. One idea per post.
- No hashtags. No em-dashes.
- Last post: soft CTA or a question to drive replies.

## LinkedIn
- Hook line, then a blank line, then the body (buyer psychology: current
  state vs. new state where it fits).
- Short paragraphs, generous white space, easy to scan on mobile.
- Story-driven or lesson-driven. 1-3 hashtags max at the very end.
- Clear CTA (comment a keyword, DM, or a link).

## Instagram Reel
- Give 3 hook options (first 2-3 seconds, spoken + on-screen text).
- 30-60 second spoken script.
- Mark on-screen text cues in [brackets].
- End with a caption + CTA + 3-5 relevant hashtags.

## TikTok
- Casual, native, unpolished tone. Hook must land in the first 2 seconds.
- Give 3 hook options.
- 20-45 second script with a trending-audio note if relevant.
- Caption short and human.

## YouTube Short
- Vertical script, 30-60s, hook first, high retention.
- On-screen text cues in [brackets].

## YouTube (long form)
- 5 title options (curiosity gap, benefit-first, contrarian, number-based).
- 1 thumbnail concept (layout + text + vibe).
- Chapter list / outline.

## Newsletter (email)
- 5 subject line options (short, punchy, curiosity or bold stance).
- Body: hook, story/value, one CTA.

## Community post (Discord / Skool / Circle)
- Casual, direct, community voice. A discussion prompt or value drop.
- End with a question to spark replies.
